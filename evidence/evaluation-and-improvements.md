# Evaluation and Improvements

## Two Things That Work Well

1. The original Thirdy Portfolio design remains consistent across Home, About, Contact, and Address, including the dark red background, coral-red accents, cards, images, buttons, and hover effects.
2. The new music player is fully interactive and adds audio multimedia without changing the original layout concept. It supports playlist selection, play/pause, next/previous, seeking, volume, automatic track advance, and Spotify links.

## Two Problems / Weaknesses Identified

1. The previous version added a large Preliminary Exam section and Exam navigation/button that made the personal portfolio look more like a school checklist than the original portfolio.
2. The previous Gaming card used large gaming screenshots and did not provide an audio multimedia feature.

## Two Improvements Made

1. The Exam navigation, Exam Requirements button, visible Preliminary Exam section, and video player were removed. The required documentation and evidence were moved to supporting files instead.
2. The Gaming card was replaced with a compact Spotify-inspired music player using optimized audio loading through `preload="metadata"`, while the original Contact and Address pages were restored from the public GitHub portfolio.

## Evidence of Improvement

The `evidence/before/` folder contains screenshots of the previous version showing:

- the Exam Requirements button;
- the large Exam Requirements section;
- the previous exam-tab layout; and
- the old Gaming card.

The current `about.html`, `contact.html`, and `address.html` files show the improved version.
